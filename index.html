Enhancing DoorDash Revenue in Optimizing Delivery Efficiency

Graduate capstone · Mercer University · March–May 2024
Advisor: Professor Rui Sun

I worked with one classmate to clean and preprocess 197,429 records and compare seven models for predicting total delivery time: LASSO regression, multiple linear regression, decision trees, random forests, support vector regression, XGBoost, and LightGBM.
